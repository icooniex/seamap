Problem Statement

The rapid growth of e-commerce has led to an exponential increase in packaging waste, with most current solutions being non-biodegradable and contributing significantly to environmental pollution.

Current Challenges:
  • Over 165 billion packages are shipped annually worldwide
  • Most packaging materials are single-use and non-recyclable
  • Significant carbon footprint from packaging production and disposal
  • Limited sustainable alternatives that maintain product protection
  • Cost considerations preventing widespread adoption of eco-friendly options

What We're Looking For

Biodegradable Materials

Innovative materials that decompose naturally within 6-12 months without harmful residues

Product Protection

Solutions that maintain or improve current protection standards for fragile items

Reusable Systems

Circular packaging solutions that can be returned and reused multiple times

Cost-Effective

Solutions that are economically viable for large-scale implementation

Technical Requirements

Material Properties
  • Biodegradable within 12 months in industrial composting
  • Water-resistant for shipping protection
  • Temperature stable (-20°C to 60°C)
  • Minimum tensile strength of 30 MPa
Performance Standards
  • ISTA 6-Amazon.com-SIOC certification
  • Drop test compliance (1.2m height)
  • Compression resistance (250 lbs)
  • Moisture barrier (WVTR ≤ 1.0 g/m²/day)
Sustainability Metrics
  • Carbon footprint reduction ≥ 50%
  • Renewable content ≥ 80%
  • End-of-life biodegradability certification
  • LCA assessment documentation
