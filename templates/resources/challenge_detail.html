Challenge Overview

Ocean plastic pollution has become one of the most pressing environmental challenges of our time. Every year, millions of tons of plastic waste end up in our oceans, threatening marine life and ecosystems worldwide.

We are seeking innovative solutions that can significantly reduce ocean plastic waste through:

  • Revolutionary recycling technologies
  • Biodegradable packaging alternatives
  • Ocean cleanup mechanisms
  • Consumer behavior change initiatives
  • Supply chain optimization solutions

Your solution should demonstrate scalability, sustainability, and measurable impact on reducing ocean plastic pollution.

Challenge Categories:
Marine Conservation Clean Technology Sustainability Innovation Recycling & Waste Ocean Health Environmental Impact Circular Economy

Requirements & Criteria

Innovation Requirements

Your solution must demonstrate novel approach or significant improvement over existing methods. We are looking for breakthrough technologies that can make a measurable impact on ocean plastic pollution.

Technical Criteria

  • Scalability: Proof of concept for scaling to global or regional implementation
  • Environmental Impact: Measurable reduction in ocean plastic waste with sustainability assessment
  • Technical Feasibility: Clear implementation plan with realistic timeline and resource requirements
  • Cost Effectiveness: Economic viability and potential for commercial adoption

Submission Requirements

All submissions must include the following components:

  1. Detailed project proposal (max 20 pages)
  2. Technical specifications and methodology
  3. Environmental impact assessment
  4. Implementation timeline and milestones
  5. Budget breakdown and funding requirements
Important Note: Late submissions will not be accepted. Please ensure all materials are submitted before the deadline.

Evaluation Criteria

Projects will be evaluated based on:

  • Innovation and creativity (30%)
  • Technical feasibility (25%)
  • Environmental impact potential (25%)
  • Scalability and commercial viability (20%)

For more detailed guidelines, please download the Challenge Brief PDF from the resources section.
