Challenge Overview

Ocean plastic pollution has become one of the most pressing environmental challenges of our time. Every year, millions of tons of plastic waste end up in our oceans, threatening marine life and ecosystems worldwide.

We are seeking innovative solutions that can significantly reduce ocean plastic waste through:

  • Revolutionary recycling technologies
  • Biodegradable packaging alternatives
  • Ocean cleanup mechanisms
  • Consumer behavior change initiatives
  • Supply chain optimization solutions

Your solution should demonstrate scalability, sustainability, and measurable impact on reducing ocean plastic pollution.

Requirements & Criteria

Innovation Level

Solution must demonstrate novel approach or significant improvement over existing methods

Scalability

Proof of concept for scaling to global or regional implementation

Environmental Impact

Measurable reduction in ocean plastic waste with sustainability assessment

Technical Feasibility

Clear implementation plan with realistic timeline and resource requirements
