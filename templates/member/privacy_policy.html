11. Cookies

The SEA-MaP Marketplace uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze site usage, and support our platform’s functionality. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us recognize you, remember your preferences, and improve our services.

We use the following types of cookies on our platform:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the operation of our website, such as enabling secure logins and core site functionality. These cannot be disabled.
  • Performance Cookies: Collect information about how you use our website (e.g., which pages you visit most often) to help us improve site performance and user experience.
  • Functionality Cookies: Allow our website to remember your preferences (such as language or region) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.
  • Analytics Cookies: We use third-party analytics services, including Google Analytics, to collect and analyze information about your interaction with our platform. This helps us understand usage patterns and improve our services. Google Analytics cookies may collect your IP address and usage data. For more information, see How Google uses data.
  • Tag Management Cookies: We use Google Tag Manager to manage website tags and scripts. Google Tag Manager itself does not collect personal data, but it may trigger other tags that do.
  • Social Media Cookies: Our platform may include social media features or tags (such as Facebook Pixel) that allow you to share content or interact with social networks. These cookies may track your browsing across other sites and build a profile of your interests.
  • Advertising/Targeting Cookies: Used to deliver advertisements relevant to you and your interests, including limiting the number of times you see an ad and measuring ad campaign effectiveness.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent tool. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and features of our platform.
