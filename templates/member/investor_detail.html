About Blue Ocean Fund
Overview

Blue Ocean Fund is a Singapore-based impact investment fund that focuses on ocean conservation and marine technology. Founded in 2018, the fund aims to support startups and projects that address ocean plastic pollution, sustainable fishing, and marine biodiversity conservation in Southeast Asia.

Investment Thesis

Our investment thesis centers on the belief that protecting our oceans requires innovative technologies and sustainable business models. We invest in companies that demonstrably reduce marine pollution, promote sustainable use of marine resources, or help communities adapt to climate change impacts on coastal areas.
