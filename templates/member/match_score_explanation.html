HOW MATCH SCORE WORKS

Understanding how SEA-MAP calculates compatibility between members

The Match Score helps you quickly identify the most compatible partners on the SEA-MAP platform. It is calculated automatically using your company profile information and the profile of each listed company.

1. Who Sees a Match Score?

Match scores are only shown when there is a meaningful relationship between the viewer and the listed company types. The table below summarises when a score is displayed:

Scores are hidden when viewing your own type (e.g. startup browsing startups) or for unsupported combinations (e.g. investor browsing corporates), because no meaningful compatibility algorithm applies.

2. Investor ↔ Startup Score (out of 100)

When an investor views startups (or a startup views investors), the score is calculated from four criteria:

Investment Categories overlap 40 pts

Compares the investor's preferred investment categories against the startup's industry categories. Full overlap = 40 pts. Partial overlap = proportional score. If the investor has not specified categories, a neutral 20 pts is awarded.

Funding Stage compatibility 30 pts

Checks whether the investor's preferred funding stages (e.g. Seed, Series A) include the startup's current stage. A full match = 30 pts. If the investor has no stage preferences set, a neutral 15 pts is awarded.

Geographic market overlap 20 pts

Compares the investor's target markets with the startup's primary location. A country match = 20 pts. If the investor has no market preferences, a neutral 10 pts is awarded.

Deal size compatibility 10 pts

Checks whether the investor's average deal size is compatible with the startup's funding requirement. Compatible ranges = 10 pts. If either party has not specified an amount, a neutral 5 pts is awarded.

3. Corporate ↔ Startup Score (out of 100)

When a corporate views startups (or a startup views corporates), the score is calculated from three criteria:

Support areas overlap 50 pts

Compares the corporate's support areas (e.g. mentoring, market access, technology) against the types of support the startup is seeking. Full overlap = 50 pts. Partial overlap = proportional score. Neutral 25 pts if the corporate has no areas specified.

Innovation types overlap 30 pts

Compares the corporate's innovation interests against the startup's categories or innovation types. Full overlap = 30 pts. Partial overlap = proportional score. Neutral 15 pts if the corporate has nothing specified.

Geographic market overlap 20 pts

Compares the corporate's target markets with the startup's primary location. A country match = 20 pts. Neutral 10 pts if no markets are specified.

4. Neutral Partial Credit

To avoid penalising companies with incomplete profiles, each criterion awards a neutral partial score (typically half its maximum value) when the viewer has not yet filled in that particular preference field. This means a new or partially complete profile will still generate a meaningful score, reflecting pure uncertainty rather than a poor match.

We encourage all members to complete their profiles for more accurate and personalised match results.

5. Score Range

All scores are on a 0 – 100 scale. A score of 80 or above is considered a High Match and is highlighted on both the dashboards and the company profile pages. The score is recalculated in real time each time you load a dashboard or profile page, reflecting the latest profile data of both parties.

If you have questions about the scoring or believe a score is inaccurate, please contact us.
