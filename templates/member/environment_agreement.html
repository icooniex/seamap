Environment & Social Participation Agreement

Effective Date: October 15, 2025

The SEA-MaP Regional Platform for Innovation & Investments is committed to promoting environmentally sound, socially inclusive and responsible innovation across Southeast Asia. All activities under the Platform are expected to be consistent with the World Bank’s Environmental and Social Framework (ESF).

By using the SEA-MaP Regional Platform for Innovation & Investments and related services, you acknowledge and support this commitment. You agree to:

  • conduct your activities in line with applicable national laws and good international practices;
  • promote fair and safe working conditions and non-discrimination;
  • support resource-efficient and environmentally responsible solutions; and
  • engage with other users transparently, respectfully and without misuse of the Platform.

The SEA-MaP Regional Platform for Innovation & Investments maintains a Grievance Redress Mechanism (GRM) for any person who wishes to raise a concern or provide feedback on environmental or social matters related to SEA-MaP activities. You may submit a grievance at any time through the GRM channels indicated on this website. All submissions will be handled confidentially and in line with the GRM procedure, and raising a grievance will not affect your access to, or standing within, the Platform.
