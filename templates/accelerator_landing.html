Eligibility Criteria

Passionate entrepreneurs with innovative plastic circularity solutions.

  • Startups focused on plastic circularity technology
  • Technology-driven solutions with clear environmental impact and commercial potential
  • Based in ASEAN region or committed to establishing a presence in Southeast Asia
  • Team of 2-5 co-founders with relevant expertise and complementary skills
  • Prototype or MVP ready for testing, validation, and further development
  • Full commitment to participate in the 6-month intensive program
Application Requirements
  • • Complete application form with company and team details
  • • 3-minute pitch video demonstrating your solution
  • • Business plan or executive summary (max 10 pages)
  • • Financial projections and funding requirements
  • • Letters of support or early customer validation
