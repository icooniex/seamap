## Introduction

Welcome to the **SeaMap Regional Platform for Innovation and Investments**! This platform connects startups, corporates, investors, policymakers, and civil society to foster innovation in plastic circularity and waste management across Southeast Asia.

### What is SeaMap?

The Southeast Asia Regional Program on Combating Marine Plastics (SEA-MaP) is a five-year initiative funded by the World Bank and implemented by the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) through the ASEAN Secretariat. The platform supports partnerships that accelerate plastic circularity innovations across Southeast Asia.

### Key Features

- **Intelligent Matchmaking**: Connect with relevant partners based on your interests and criteria
- **Multi-User Types**: Tailored experiences for startups, investors, and corporates
- **Comprehensive Profiles**: Detailed company and personal profiles for better matching
- **Document Management**: Secure storage and sharing of important documents
- **Advanced Search & Filtering**: Find exactly what you're looking for
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: Enhanced security for your account
